## Ticket Lifecycle Overview

![Ticket Lifecycle Overview](screenshots/Ticket%20Lifecycle%20Overview.png)

### Ticket States

- Open: a new ticket is submitted by a client via the osTicket guest portal and is waiting to be assigned to an agent.
- Assigned: osTicket routes that ticket to the appropriate department and are assigned to an agent. 
- In Progress: Active 
- On Hold
- Resolved
- Closed
